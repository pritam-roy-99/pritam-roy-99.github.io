About

Physics, information, and operational questions.

I am a Senior Research Fellow and Ph.D. researcher at S. N. Bose National Centre for Basic Sciences, Kolkata, working in quantum information theory and quantum foundations.

Profile

Pritam Roy

My research focuses on operational certification and application of quantum resources. I work across preparation contextuality, device-independent and one-sided device-independent cryptography, sequential quantum measurements, encrypted recovery, open quantum systems, and optomechanical quantum physics.

Across these topics, I am especially interested in results that have both a structural statement and an operational consequence: an exact resource criterion, a security threshold, a communication advantage, or a protocol that can in principle be tested.
