01 Alice prepares her qubits

For every qubit, Alice picks a random bit and a random basis (rectilinear or diagonal), preparing the photon in one of |0⟩, |1⟩, |+⟩, |−⟩. Only she knows both values right now.

02 Photons travel down the quantum channel

Each photon travels from Alice to Bob. Watch what happens when Eve is present.

03 Bob measures each photon

Bob has no idea which basis Alice used, so he guesses randomly for each photon. When his guess matches, he recovers the bit perfectly; when it doesn't, the result is a coin flip.

04 Public basis reconciliation (sifting)

Over an ordinary public channel, Alice and Bob announce only their bases — never their bits. Wherever the bases match, the bit is kept as part of the sifted key; everywhere else, it's discarded.

05 Estimating the error rate & extracting the key

Alice and Bob publicly reveal a random sample of their sifted bits to check for disturbance. A low error rate means the channel — and the rest of the sifted key — can be trusted.
