Research programme

Three branches, one operational question.

I work on quantum resources from three directions: foundational certification, secure information processing, and dynamics in open or macroscopic systems. Across them, the recurring aim is to turn abstract nonclassical structure into a concrete operational statement.

Quantum Foundations & Certification

Which nonclassical resource is responsible for an operational advantage, and how little structure must we trust to certify it?

I study preparation contextuality, exclusion-based communication tasks, sequential measurements, and multipartite resource structure. These problems turn foundational notions into experimentally meaningful witnesses, communication advantages, or exact resource criteria.

Preparation contextuality & PORECExclusion-type tasks where the classical/noncontextual boundary differs sharply from ordinary random-access coding.
Sequential quantum instrumentsOperational certification of instrument quality from multi-observer communication games.
Multipartite entanglement structureCollectivity requirements and resource constraints for broadcasting or recovering genuinely multipartite quantum correlations.
Measurement contextualityHow POVM asymmetry, joint measurements, and measurement geometry influence contextual advantage.

Quantum cryptography

Quantum Cryptography & Secure Information

How far can security survive when devices, channels, or intermediate operations cannot be fully trusted?

My cryptography work spans fully device-independent and one-sided device-independent QKD, secure direct communication in noisy channels, sequential attacks, and exact encrypted recovery. The common theme is security from observable correlations and resource structure rather than idealised device assumptions.

DI & 1sDI quantum key distributionBell nonlocality and steering as security resources under collective and sequential attacks.
Encrypted quantum cloningExact multipartite resource criteria for concealed signals that can be selectively decrypted.
Secure direct communicationDevice-independent communication in non-Markovian channels.
Interactive cryptography labsBrowser simulations that expose assumptions, statistics, and abort conditions step by step.

Open Quantum Systems & Optomechanical Physics

Can environmental memory and measurement back-action be controlled so that fragile quantum behaviour becomes easier to observe?

This branch connects quantum information to nonequilibrium dynamics and massive systems. I study non-Markovian information flow, reset-driven harmonic chains, and measurement sequences that amplify observable nonclassicality through the quantum Zeno effect.

Non-Markovian quantum channelsMemory effects as both a dynamical phenomenon and a cryptographic resource or limitation.
Boundary-reset harmonic chainsStationary states and nonequilibrium transport driven by stochastic reset processes.
Quantum Zeno amplificationRepeated no-click measurements designed to amplify macroscopic nonclassical signatures.
Optomechanical implementationRealistic beam-splitter interactions, optical loss, mechanical damping, and experimentally constrained protocols.
