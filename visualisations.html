Concept map

The trust spectrum.

The library is ordered by the assumptions required for security, not by historical date.

Trusted devices

BB84 · B92

Security uses a specified preparation and measurement model. The key signatures are sifting, nonorthogonality, and QBER.

One-sided trust

1sDI-QKD

Only Bob is trusted. Steering constrains the adversary and bridges device-dependent and fully DI security.

Black-box devices

E91 · DI-QKD

Bell-nonlocal statistics certify correlations without relying on a detailed internal device model.

increasing device independence → stronger certification assumptions are replaced by observed correlations
